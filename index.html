About

From Operations to Information Systems

My professional interests sit at the intersection of supply chain operations, information systems, GIS and data.

Working in supply chain operations has given me practical exposure to how inventory, documentation, transportation, people and information have to work together. At the same time, my Information Systems Engineering studies have developed my interest in databases, software, data and analytical systems. My current role gives me exposure to real operational challenges, while my studies and technical projects allow me to explore how technology and data can improve those processes.
